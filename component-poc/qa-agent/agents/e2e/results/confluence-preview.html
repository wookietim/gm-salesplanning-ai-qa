Detail for all 3 findings

LIVE-001 — "Gap to close" shows on HFBs that beat their goal and is hidden on the worst performers

Observed: On the US region dashboard with the Qty index toggle selected, HFB 10 - Lighting & Home electronics (index 111, +11pt ABOVE goal) displays "Gap to close: 201K / $49.8M", while HFB 17 - Outdoor (index 73, -27pt BELOW goal) displays no gap line at all. The same inversion holds across the list: every above-goal HFB shows a gap, no below-goal HFB does.

Expected: The "Gap to close" line should appear for HFBs that are short of goal — those are the ones with a gap to close — and be hidden once the goal is met.

Why it matters: Planners use this list to decide where to act. As rendered, the affordance points them at the HFBs that are already ahead and stays silent on the ones furthest behind, which is the opposite of the intended 'By gap to goal (worst first)' framing of the section.

Source evidence: src/components/GapToClose/gap-to-close.tsx documents "positive input values represent a missing gap and negative values represent a fulfilled gap", but the visibility rule is `if (size === 'large' && netQuantityGap >= 0) return null;` commented "the goal has been met". Those two statements are mutually contradictory, and the usage example ("pass negative numbers to show a gap") contradicts the first one again. netQuantityGap comes straight from the API (sales-performance/api.ts), so the fix needs the backend sign convention confirmed before changing the component — do not simply flip the comparison.

LIVE-002 — "vs goal" badge disappears entirely when the delta is exactly zero

Observed: HFB 15 - Eating renders a sales index of exactly 100 (0pt vs goal). Its "vs goal" badge is not empty — it is absent from the DOM entirely (querySelectorAll('.badge') returns []). Every other HFB card in the list renders a badge.

Expected: A zero delta is a real, meaningful result (exactly on goal) and should render a badge showing 0pt, presumably in a neutral colour.

Why it matters: The card silently loses a metric. A reader cannot distinguish 'exactly on goal' from 'data unavailable', and the row reads as broken next to its populated peers.

Only reproducible against real data — a value landing on exactly 100 does not occur in the E2E mock fixtures, so no automated test covers this state.

LIVE-003 — Direction of "vs goal" is conveyed by an aria-hidden icon at country level, losing the sign for screen readers

Observed: The country-level card renders "vs goal" as an amber badge whose direction is carried by an <svg> arrow marked aria-hidden="true"; its text content is the bare string "6pt" with no sign. HFB-level cards instead render a literal text minus, e.g. "-27pt", in a red badge.

Expected: Both levels should express direction consistently, and the sign should be present in the accessible name rather than encoded only in a decorative icon.

Why it matters: Screen reader and text-extraction users hear "6pt" at country level with no indication of whether the region is above or below goal — a reversal of meaning, not just a loss of nuance. The inconsistency between the two levels also makes the UI harder to read visually.

Surfaced while cross-checking LIVE-001: the missing sign initially made the country card look as though it disagreed with the HFB cards.
